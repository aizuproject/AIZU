# Community code of conduct

AIZU welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work respectfully and explain technical disagreements with evidence.
- Make room for questions, language differences and mistakes.
- Give useful, specific feedback and acknowledge corrections.
- Respect privacy and the right of others to decline further interaction.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory or sexualized abuse, threats, repeated unwanted contact, impersonation, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Do not use issues or reviews to target individuals or pressure them into disclosing personal details.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close a discussion, issue a warning, or restrict participation according to the severity and pattern of behavior. They are expected to apply this policy consistently, including to their own participation.

Do not post private evidence in a public issue. GitHub's reporting controls can be used for abusive content or account behavior on the platform. Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).
